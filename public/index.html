Knowledge base

Drug–gene pairs we cover

Opioid

Codeine

CYP2D6

Prodrug activated to morphine; poor metabolizers get little analgesia, ultrarapid metabolizers risk toxicity.

Antiplatelet

Clopidogrel

CYP2C19

Requires CYP2C19 activation; reduced function lowers platelet inhibition and raises stent-thrombosis risk.

Anticoagulant

Warfarin

CYP2C9

CYP2C9 clears S-warfarin; reduced activity increases exposure and bleeding risk, needing lower doses.

Statin

Simvastatin

SLCO1B1

SLCO1B1 transports the drug into the liver; reduced function raises myopathy risk.

Immunosuppressant

Azathioprine

TPMT

TPMT inactivates toxic metabolites; low activity can cause severe myelotoxicity.

Chemotherapy

Fluorouracil

DPYD

DPYD catabolises most of the dose; reduced activity leads to severe, potentially fatal toxicity.
